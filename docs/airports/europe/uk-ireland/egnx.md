| Designator | Length | Cat |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| **09 / 27** | 2,893 m | CAT II |

### Preferential Runway

Runway **27** preferred in westerly winds. East Midlands has significant cargo operations (DHL, UPS, Royal Mail) with heavy freighter movements particularly overnight.
