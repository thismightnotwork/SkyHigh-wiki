## Local Regulations

- All inbound aircraft must report **type, latest ATIS and cleared level** on first contact with Manchester Radar.
- All flights subject to **PPR**. Filing a flight plan does NOT constitute permission to use Manchester.
- PPR/slot requests: ACL Tel: 0161-493 1850 (office hours) | Airfield Ops 0161-489 3657 (out of hours).
- **A-CDM in operation.** Pilots must call Manchester Delivery for ATC clearance **10–15 min before start**. Include aircraft type, stand number and ATIS letter.
- **Push-back approval given on GMC frequency only** — start-up approval does NOT imply push-back approval.
- Pilots must report READY at TOBT (tolerance ±5 min). Failure to do so will result in TOBT/TSAT deletion.
- Push and hold procedures in operation for CTOT-delayed flights.
- **DCL/CPDLC** available for suitably equipped aircraft.
- **Taxiway November-Charlie** has painted centreline only — after sunset or during LVP, use only with a Follow-Me service.
- **Taxiways Zulu, November-Alpha, November-Bravo** have three centrelines (central + Blue/Orange for Code C). Follow relevant centreline as instructed by ATC.
- Aircraft with wingspan >36 M arriving 0300–0800 should plan arrival per SIBT due to capacity.
- Stopbars H24 at all runway holding points.
- **RFF Category A10.**
