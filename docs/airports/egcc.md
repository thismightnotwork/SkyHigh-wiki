## Local Notes

- Manchester is a slot-coordinated airport — commercial movements require ACL slot approval.
- Both runways support CAT III operations independently.
- CDA techniques are expected for all turbo-jet/prop aircraft.
- Parallel runway operations are in use; ATC will specify the active runway configuration.
