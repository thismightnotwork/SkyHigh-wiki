## Local Notes

- Operations are H24; all movements require ATC clearance.
- Heathrow is a slot-coordinated airport — all commercial movements require ACL slot approval.
- CAT II/III Low Visibility Procedures (LVP) are regularly in force.
- Continuous Descent Approaches (CDA) are mandatory for all turbo-jet/prop aircraft.
- SVFR is permitted but subject to ATC approval.
- All telephone calls to NATS operational areas may be recorded.
