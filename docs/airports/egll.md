## Local Regulations

- All inbound aircraft must report **type, latest ATIS letter and cleared level** on first contact with Heathrow Radar.
- **Easterly Operations** use 09L/09R; **Westerly Operations** use 27L/27R. Runway alternation scheme applies. Normally the southern runway (27L/09R) is used for landings during westerly ops.
- Segregated parallel runway operations are standard. Independent simultaneous approaches are not normally in use.
- **CAT II/III** operations approved on all four runway ends.
- All stands nose-in push-back only. Fixed Electrical Ground Power must be used wherever available.
- **DCL** (Data Clearance Link) available for suitably equipped aircraft.
- Pilots must report aircraft type, stand number and ATIS letter on first call to ATC.
