## Local Regulations

- **All flights** (including GA and Military) require prior approval from Operations Director, Glasgow Airport Ltd, and notification to ACL. Ad-hoc slots: ACL 0161-493 1850 (0830–1700 Mon–Fri office hours) or GLAL Duty Manager 0141-848-4510 / 07831-170676 (out of hours, short-term only).
- No aircraft permitted to park for **>24 hours** without prior approval.
- **Microlights and gyrocopters NOT permitted** unless emergency.
- Departing aircraft on first contact must state **type, stand number and ATIS letter**. ATC clearance available from EOBT–15 min.
- **DCL available** (ARINC 623-2 compliant). Available EOBT–25 to EOBT+15 min. Must accept within 5 min or **REVERT TO VOICE**. Do NOT use free text option.
- All stands nose-in push-back. Push-back must face direction of taxi before commencing.
- Before calling for push-back, commander must ensure **tug driver is in the tug and ready**. ATC will specify push direction at stands with two options (stands 6, 8, 19, 21, 30L, 30, 30R, 32, 35, 37, 40).
- **A-VDGS** fitted on stands 3–5, 9–11, 14–40. AGNIS on stands 1, 1A, 2, 12, 64, 65, 81, 82. Marshalling on stands 6, 6A, 7, 8, 31, 61–63. Do NOT self-park if A-VDGS not activated or no marshaller present.
- **Taxiway Charlie is CLOSED to all arriving and departing aircraft.**
- **CAT II/III** approved on Runway 05 and 23. LVP holding points: A2 (RWY 23), G2 (RWY 05). During CAT II/III, A380 holds at A3 and G2.
- **A380 operations:** Stand 30 preferred (twin upper + lower bridge). Stand 37 contingency (tow to stand only). Reduced centreline-to-object clearance of 47.5 M on sections of Alpha/Golf taxiways. Code E/F cannot 180° turn on runway.
- Section of runway between RWY 23 THR and Link F exhibits **fast drying capability** after wet conditions (extends 8.5 M either side of CL).
- **RFF Category A9** — upgraded to A10 daily 1100–1500 local. Upgrades outside these hours available via PPR through ACL or Duty Manager.
- Grass verges soft — exercise caution when taxiing near edges.

### Bird Warning

Large flocks of **Greylag/Canada Geese and Whooper Swans** Sep–Apr, regularly >100 birds, up to 500 FT AGL. Active bird control in operation at all times.
