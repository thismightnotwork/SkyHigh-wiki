## Local Notes

- All flights (including GA and military) require prior approval from Glasgow Airport Ltd and ACL slot coordination.
- DCL (Data Clearance Link) available for suitably equipped aircraft (EOBT−25 to EOBT+15).
- CAT II/III LVP operations apply to RWY 05/23; CAT II/III holds at Links A2 and G2.
- A340-600 is **not** permitted at Glasgow Airport. Microlights and gyrocopters are prohibited except in emergency.
- Aircraft requiring full length of RWY 23 must back-track via Hold B1; Code E aircraft cannot execute 180° turns on RWY 05/23.
- Noise limits: 94 dBA day (0600–2330) / 87 dBA night (2330–0600). Chapter 3 non-compliant jet aircraft not permitted 2330–0559 without Managing Director approval.
- Large flocks of Greylag/Canada Geese and Whooper Swans present Sep–Apr; active bird control in operation.
