## Runways

| Designator | Dimensions | Surface | ILS Cat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 08R / 26L | 3316 × 46 M | Asphalt, Grooved | CAT III |
| 08L / 26R | 2565 × 46 M | Asphalt, Grooved | CAT I (standby) |

!!! note
    Gatwick normally operates as a single-runway airport using 08R/26L. The northern runway 08L/26R is a standby runway used only when the main runway is unavailable.

## Local Notes

- Gatwick is a slot-coordinated airport — all commercial movements require ACL slot approval.
- CAT II/III LVP operations are regularly applied to RWY 08R/26L.
- CDA techniques are mandatory for all turbo-jet/prop aircraft.
- The standby runway (08L/26R) requires prior coordination with ATC before use.
