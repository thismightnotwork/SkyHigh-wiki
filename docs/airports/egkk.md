## Local Regulations

- Gatwick operates **single runway operations** in normal conditions (08L/26R). The standby runway (08R/26L) is available in contingency.
- All inbound aircraft must report **type, ATIS letter and cleared level** on first contact.
- PPR required for all general aviation movements.
- **CAT II/III** approved on 26R and 08L.
- **DCL** available for suitably equipped aircraft.
- All stands nose-in push-back. Fixed Electrical Ground Power must be used wherever available.
- Night noise restrictions apply — aircraft must not exceed Chapter 3 standards.
