# EGSS London Stansted
## SKYHIGH UK Aerodrome Procedures Manual

>Not for real-world operations

---

## PRE — Preface

- This manual is for controllers operating at EGSS Aerodrome and Approach positions.
- Must be used alongside:
  - UK MATS Part 1 (CAP493)
  - CAP413 Radiotelephony procedures
- Applies to:
  - Aerodrome Control (DEL / GND / TWR)
  - Approach Control (APP / DIR where applicable)

---

## GEN — General Operating Procedures

### 1. Controller Responsibilities
- Maintain separation and flow 
- Ensure coordination with adjacent units (London TMA / EGTT sectors)
- Use standard phraseology per CAP413


### 2. Communication Standards
- All transmissions must be concise and standardised
- Use ICAO phonetics and UK RTF phraseology
- Avoid non-standard instructions unless necessary for safety/flow

### 3. Coordination Requirements
- Level changes must be coordinated before transfer
- Route amendments must be agreed prior to handoff
- Coordination may be electronic or verbal depending on facility

---

## ADC — Aerodrome Control Procedures (EGSS Tower & Ground)

### 3.1 Runway Operations
- Primary runway configuration: **07/25 (subject to wind)**
- Runway usage determined by:
  - Wind direction/speed
  - ATC flow management
  - Noise abatement procedures

### 3.2 Taxi Procedures
- Standard taxi clearance required before movement
- Ground control responsible for:
  - Stand allocation
  - Taxi routing
- Pilots must hold short of active runway unless cleared

### 3.3 Departures
- Departure clearances include:
  - SID (Standard Instrument Departure)
  - Initial climb instruction
  - Squawk code (if applicable)

- Coordination required with Approach for:
  - Runway changes
  - Non-standard departures

### 3.4 Arrivals
- Tower responsible for landing clearance only
- Approach provides sequencing and ILS/vectors
- Aircraft transferred to Tower on final approach

---

## APC — Approach Control Procedures (EGSS APP / EGSS Director)

### 4.1 General Approach Handling
- Stansted arrivals are sequenced by EGSS APP
- Radar vectors used for:
  - Sequencing
  - Separation
  - Final approach integration

### 4.2 STAR Procedures
- Standard STARs used unless otherwise instructed by ATC
- Aircraft must comply with:
  - Published altitude restrictions
  - Speed constraints (where applicable)

### 4.3 Separation Standards
- Radar separation applied:
  - Minimum 3–5 NM depending on phase
- Vertical separation used during sequencing when required

### 4.4 Transfer of Control
- Transfer points defined by:
  - Final approach fix (or equivalent)
  - Sector boundary
- Communication transfer must occur prior to handoff point

---

## SEP — Separation & Coordination Rules

### 5.1 Separation Types
- Radar separation (primary method)
- Vertical separation when radar not available or required
- Wake turbulence separation (heavy/medium/light aircraft rules)

### 5.2 Coordination Rules
- All level changes must be agreed before execution
- All runway changes must be coordinated between APP and TWR
- Departures must be coordinated if they affect inbound sequencing
