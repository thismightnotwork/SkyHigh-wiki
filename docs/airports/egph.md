## Local Notes

- All operators must pre-arrange a handling agent; all aircraft are PPR with their agent.
- DCL (Data Clearance Link) is available for suitably equipped aircraft (EOBT−25 to EOBT+15).
- No visual approaches permitted for IFR aircraft between 2230–0630 local; ILS approaches under ATC control only.
- Noise monitoring terminals at Livingston, Broxburn and Cramond; 94 dBA day / 87 dBA night limits apply.
- Code E/F aircraft must vacate the runway at the end (via A1 or D1) unless escorted by follow-me vehicle.
- CDA procedures apply from ALT 6000 FT QNH.
- Large bird populations around the aerodrome — expect active bird deterrent activity.
