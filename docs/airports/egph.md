## Local Regulations

- All flights (except GA and Military) require prior approval from EAL CEO and notification to ACL. Ad-hoc slots: ACL 0161-493 1850 (office hours) or EAL Airside Ops 0131-344 3139 (other times).
- A **licensed EAL handling agent is mandatory** for all flights including GA, cargo, military and helicopters.
- Departing aircraft on first contact must state **type, stand number and ATIS letter**.
- ATC clearance can be requested from EOBT–25 min. Crews should have departure clearance **before** requesting push and start.
- **DCL available.** Must be accepted within 5 min or **REVERT TO VOICE** is issued. Do not use free text.
- Anti-collision beacons should only be switched on **after** start clearance is received.
- Flight crew/cabin crew in airside external areas must wear a **fully fastened high-vis jacket/tabard**.
- **Code E/F aircraft** must vacate runway at runway end (via A1 or D1) unless escorted by Follow-Me.
- Standard Taxi Routes after landing: A1→A8 via TWY A; B1→A12 via TWY B+A; C1→A15 or A16; D1→A18 via TWY D+A.
- **No intersection departures** from Bravo and Charlie during CAT II/III.
- **CAT II/III** approved on Runway 06 and 24.
- Passing places (V and W loops) at either end of TWY Alpha — aircraft may pass only when both have wingspan <36 M.
- Large bird population — expect birdstrike deterrent activity (shell crackers).
- **RFF Category A9.**

### Noise Abatement

- Max **94 dBA Lmax** day (0600–2330) | Max **87 dBA Lmax** night (2330–0600)
- NPRs must be strictly adhered to. No visual IFR approaches 2230–0630.
